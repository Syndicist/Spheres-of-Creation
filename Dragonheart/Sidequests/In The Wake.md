After a recent storm, a badly broken merchant vessel has washed ashore along the wreck-strewn coastline north of town. Kelven Dorr believes it may carry valuable cargo—and more importantly, wants to ensure nothing dangerous comes with it.

Kelven acts as a laison to the shipping guild which owned the vessel, offering a modest bounty for recovered trade goods, with a bonus if the ship’s manifest or “ledger” is found. 

# The Wreck: Silverwake

The ship lies split against jagged rocks, its hull half-submerged and tilted. Waves crash through its broken frame, and the surrounding shoreline is littered with debris.

The wreck can be explored in three main sections, each with its own hazards and encounters.

## Area 1: The Splintered Deck

The upper portion of the ship is exposed, slick with seawater and unstable.

Encounter: Scavengers
2–4 reefclaws or giant crabs feeding on bodies
Alternatively: a pair of opportunistic goblins already looting

Hazard:
Acrobatics checks to avoid slipping
A collapsing mast (Reflex save or take damage)

Treasure:
1d4 minor trade goods (spices, cloth, etc.) worth 50 gp per good.
A coral-encrusted silver ring (25 gp)

## Area 2: The Flooded Hold
The lower deck is partially underwater. Entry requires swimming or careful navigation along broken beams.

Encounter: The Drowned Guard
A drowned zombie (human) or skeletal sailor
Carries rusted armor and a key tied to its belt

Optional Twist:
The undead animates only when the players disturb cargo crates.

Treasure:
Locked chest (key from the guard):
60 gp worth of mixed coin
Potion of cure moderate wounds
A sealed scroll case (waterproof)

Scroll Contents:
TBD

## Area 3: The Captain’s Cabin

The stern section remains mostly intact but precarious.

Encounter: Hidden Threat
A kobold scout lies in wait, attempting to abscong with the ledger and the lodefinder.

The Ledger is hidden beneath a false floor panel in the captain’s quarters. Alongside the ledger is a strange stone artifact that is not referenced at all in the ledger. A DC 20 Spellcraft check reveals that it is a magical device of some kind, while a DC 25 spellcraft check reveals it is a tool used for searching and lowers the UMD check to attempt to use the tool by 10. Without this knowledge, the UMD check is DC 25.

Contents of the ledger:
Names of ports and coded transactions
Repeated references to:
“Marked settlements”
“Survey shipments”
Darcey is listed—but not as a destination… as an observation point
Optional Complication

## Conclusion & Rewards
Returning the ledger to Kelven Dorr (or Maris Thorne) yields:

Standard Reward
50 gp per trade good recovered.
Salvage rights to all other recovered items.
150 Party XP

Bonus Reward
If the ledger is delivered intact:
Additional 100 gp
Favor with Darcey leadership
50 additional Party XP

GM Notes & Expansion Hooks
The ledger ties directly into the larger draconic reconnaissance network. The captain was bribed to deliver draconic cargo to a destination near Darcey - the exact details are not in the ledger.
